At the ball of Prince Metternich I had a very great satisfaction. He told me a handbillet of the Emperor had just come down concerning Meninskis Onomastikon. The resolution asks a Gutachten to be given by Hofrat Hammer. So the cabinet schools the Secretary of State’s office and the conselor of state Stifft teaches the counselor of state Stürmer his duty. It made a great sensation in the office and though it can not please Prince Metternich, I dare say it won’t hurt me.
